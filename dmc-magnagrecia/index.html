Who we are

Born in Magna Grecia. Where Europe began to travel.

Two thousand seven hundred years ago, the Greeks who settled these southern coasts became some of Europe's first travellers — and the lands they named Magna Grecia became the threshold from which Mediterranean civilization stepped outward to the world. The first hospitality, the first trade routes, the first millennial cuisine — all began here.

We did not choose Calabria by accident. We chose it because Southern Italy is a region complete: sea and mountains, vineyards and quiet villages, millennial cuisine and archaeology that predates Rome. A paradise the world has only just begun to discover.

Our team brings more than forty years of combined hospitality experience — across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East and beyond. We work from here, and our partners stand ready in Madrid, Paris, Dubai, the Caribbean, Argentina, and the United States. Wherever a journey leads, we have trusted hands on the ground.

We design two kinds of travel. We bring travellers to Magna Grecia and beyond — and we accompany those who set out from Italy toward the rest of the world. Some journeys are shaped for ten travellers together. Others are made by hand, for one. All begin with conversation.

"Italy to the world, and the world to Italy — crafted by people who have done this for forty years."

The way we work

Six things we believe.

I

Tailor-made, always.

Every itinerary is shaped to its traveller. Whether you join a small group of ten or come to us alone, the route, the rhythm, and the moments are ours to compose together.

II

Local hands, global reach.

Our team is here in Calabria. Our partners stretch from Madrid to Dubai, from the Caribbean to Argentina. Wherever the journey leads, we have someone who knows the place.

III

The "no-tour" tour.

No early-morning departures, no fifty-seater buses, no rigid schedules. Small groups, slow mornings, and the flexibility that comes from working in twos and threes rather than dozens.

IV

For every budget.

Travel made by hand is not travel made for the few. Our group tours and bespoke itineraries span every budget — what stays constant is the care, not the price.

V

Forty years of experience.

Our team brings more than four decades of combined hospitality experience — across Europe and beyond. We have been welcoming travellers since long before this house bore its name.

VI

Slow food, deep culture.

Magna Grecia gave the world millennial cuisine, ancient wines, and a way of eating the world is now rediscovering. We design journeys around the things that take time — the slow lunches, the family vineyards, the villages that have not changed.
