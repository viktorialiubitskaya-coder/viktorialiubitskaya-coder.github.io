Who We Are

A house built on two ancient ideas: hospitality, and curiosity.

We are Destination Magna Grecia — a luxury tour operator born in the lands once known as Magna Graecia, the Greek colonies of Southern Italy that gave the ancient world its philosophers, its temples, and its coins.

We design two kinds of journeys. We bring discerning travelers to Italy — guiding them through Calabria's mountain villages, Sicily's archaeological sites, the secret coast of Puglia. And we accompany Italians outward — to Japan, to Patagonia, to the deserts of Morocco — with the same eye for craft and slowness we apply at home.

We work in small numbers, by hand. Every itinerary is composed for a single traveler or family. There are no group buses, no fixed departures, no menus. Just conversations, listening, and the careful arrangement of a journey that feels inevitable from the first morning.

"The best journey is the one that feels, somehow, already yours."

The Way We Work

Six principles, no compromises.

I

Bespoke, never templated

Every journey begins with a conversation, not a brochure. We have no fixed departures, no group transfers, no copy-paste itineraries.

II

Local hands, global reach

Our team in Southern Italy is small and senior. Abroad, we work only with partners we have visited personally, often over years.

III

Slow is the point

We design for fewer destinations and longer stays. The shortest journey we recommend is seven days.

IV

Honesty about cost

Pricing is transparent and conversation-based. We do not list prices online — every journey is composed individually.

V

Cultural depth

Our guides are archaeologists, art historians, vintners, beekeepers — never scripted tour leaders.

VI

Discretion

Our travelers' identities, itineraries, and preferences remain confidential. We do not photograph for marketing without explicit permission.

Our Origin

Founded in Calabria, 2024.

Destination Magna Grecia was founded by Wilma and her partner, after two decades in luxury hospitality across Italy and abroad. The company is based in the village of Santa Domenica Talao, on the Tyrrhenian coast of Calabria — within sight of the ancient ports of the Greek colonies that gave our region its name.

We are members of Virtuoso and ASTOI Confindustria Viaggi.

— Wilma, Founder
